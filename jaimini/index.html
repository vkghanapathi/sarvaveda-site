How to read a page

Each adhikaraṇa is set as the five limbs in order. The first four are the transmitted text of Mādhava, on the Mīmāṃsā-sūtra of Jaimini, twelve adhyāyas. The fifth, saṅgati, neither author states adhikaraṇa by adhikaraṇa — both instruct that it be inferred once the topics of śāstra, adhyāya and pāda are known — so it is composed here and marked ऊहिता. The kind of avāntara-saṅgati is left open for a reading.
